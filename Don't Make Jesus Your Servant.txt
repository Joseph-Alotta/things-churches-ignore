## X. Don't Make Jesus Your Servant

- A. Don't sing and pray only when you have needs; don't make him a "bus boy"
  - Matthew 15:8-9; Luke 6:46; Luke 17:7-10 (servants and masters)
- B. Don't prove you're self-centered and outside covenant by quitting prayer when things don't go your way
  - Luke 18:1; 1 Thessalonians 5:17; Job 2:10
- C. "A thing or two to settle with God"
  - Romans 9:20; Job 40:1-5; Job 42:3-6
  1. You have nothing to settle with God; you have things to settle with your own heart
     - Jeremiah 17:9; Proverbs 4:23; Matthew 15:19
  2. If God turned up his glory in the room, we wouldn't survive it, let alone stroll into heaven with demands
     - Exodus 33:20; Isaiah 6:5; Revelation 1:17; 1 Timothy 6:16
