## IX. The Cross Means Transformation, Not Just Forgiveness

- A. The temple curtain torn top to bottom: God removed everything between himself and the people he created
  - ★ Matthew 27:51; Hebrews 10:19-20; Hebrews 9:8
- B. "Christ in you, the hope of glory"
  - ★ Colossians 1:27
  1. Glory is any made-known, realized attribute of God
     - Exodus 33:18-19 (God's glory revealed as his goodness and character); John 1:14
  2. Christ in you is the hope of God being seen, known, and realized
     - 2 Corinthians 4:6-7; 2 Corinthians 3:18
- C. How the world sees a Christ-follower
  1. When you don't fight for your rights, people think you're passive
     - 1 Peter 2:23; 1 Corinthians 6:7; Matthew 5:39
  2. God's own answer to accountability was putting his Son on the cross
     - ★ Romans 8:3; Isaiah 53:6; Romans 3:25-26
- D. Following Jesus means denying yourself and picking up your cross
  - ★ Luke 9:23; Matthew 16:24
  1. Pressing your own rights means picking up something else
     - Philippians 2:3-8
  2. That means "taking life in my own hands instead of life in me"
     - ★ Galatians 2:20; Romans 6:6
