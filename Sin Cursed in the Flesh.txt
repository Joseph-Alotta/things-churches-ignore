## XII. The Cross: Sin Cursed in the Flesh

*(Introduced as a lead-in to praying for the sick)*

- A. God didn't curse his Son; he cursed sin in the flesh
  - ★ Romans 8:3 (God condemned sin in the flesh)
  1. "He made him who knew no sin to be sin"
     - ★ 2 Corinthians 5:21
  2. Jesus became what was killing us, hung on a tree
     - ★ Galatians 3:13; Deuteronomy 21:23; John 3:14-15 (the serpent lifted up)
- B. Sin has no dominion over you, because you're under grace, not law
  - ★ Romans 6:14
- C. Grace is not a license to keep sinning
  - ★ Romans 6:1-2; Titus 2:11-12
  1. You died to sin, and your life is hidden in Christ
     - ★ Colossians 3:3; Romans 6:11
- D. What we die to: the reputation, impulse, memory, stain, resume, and history of sin
  - Romans 6:6-7; Romans 8:1; Hebrews 10:17; Isaiah 1:18
- E. Connection to praying for the sick
  - Isaiah 53:5; Matthew 8:17; 1 Peter 2:24; James 5:14-16
