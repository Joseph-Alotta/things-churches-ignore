## XI. Deny Yourself

- A. It's the one thing Jesus asks, and we make it harder than it is
  - ★ Luke 9:23; Matthew 11:28-30 (my burden is light)
  1. What it costs is only what you were never created to be
     - Philippians 3:7-8; Ephesians 4:22
  2. You're giving back a lie to receive the truth
     - Romans 1:25; John 8:32; Colossians 3:9-10
- B. A seed that doesn't die abides alone
  - ★ John 12:24-26
  1. Holding onto self-centeredness guarantees isolation
     - Luke 9:24; Proverbs 18:1
- C. The "blank canvas" life
  - Matthew 7:21-23; Revelation 3:1; 1 Corinthians 3:12-15 (works tested by fire)
  1. Church attendance, ministry, and mission trips, but with anger and selfishness underneath
     - 1 Corinthians 13:1-3; Isaiah 29:13
  2. Chasing a life that "works" instead of living for Jesus
     - Matthew 16:25-26; Luke 12:15-21
- D. Going to church doesn't build a legacy; being the church does
  - James 1:22; Ephesians 2:10; 1 Corinthians 12:27; Revelation 14:13 (their deeds follow them)
  1. Singing to Jesus doesn't fill the canvas; manifesting Jesus does, and it changes your song
     - Matthew 5:16; Psalm 40:3 ("a new song")
